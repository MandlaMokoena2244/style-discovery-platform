Shipping

Last updated: 21 November 2026. | Website: www.fashforge.co.za

FashForge ships inside South Africa only. Parcels are couriered by the brands or by FashForge. This page is part of our Terms and Conditions.

Also see our Terms and Conditions and Returns.

1. Where we deliver

We deliver anywhere in South Africa. You can have the parcel brought to your address, or collect it from a Courier Guy locker near you. We do not deliver outside South Africa. Locker collection may not be available for parcels sent directly by a brand; we'll tell you at checkout or before we confirm your order.

Some parcels are sent by FashForge. Some are sent straight to you by the brand partner, in that brand's own packaging. The goods stay at FashForge's risk until they are delivered to the address you gave us or you collect them from the locker. After that, the risk passes to you. If a brand partner sends the parcel, we remain responsible to you for loss or damage in transit.

2. Delivery fees

Door-to-door
R99. The courier brings the parcel to your address.
Courier Guy locker
R70. You choose a locker near you and collect it with a PIN within 36 hours. Locker collection may not be available for parcels sent directly by a brand; we'll tell you at checkout or before we confirm your order.
Orders of R2,000 or more
Free delivery, whether you choose door-to-door or a locker.

3. Estimated delivery time

Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days, or up to about 8 business days when the brand sends the parcel. These are estimates. If we miss the time we gave you, we will tell you, and you may wait for the parcel or cancel the delayed item for a full refund, including the delivery fee.

4. Tracking your order

We confirm your order by email, and we email you again when your parcel is on its way, with a tracking number.

5. Your address and failed deliveries

Please give a full, correct address and a phone number the courier can reach you on.

To change your address, contact us on WhatsApp or through the contact form on our website before your parcel has been sent.

Make sure someone is available to receive the parcel. Couriers usually deliver during business hours on working days.

If a delivery fails because the address is wrong or nobody is available, the courier will try to contact you. If the parcel comes back to us, we will contact you to arrange redelivery, and an extra courier fee may apply.

6. Damaged or lost parcels

Please check your parcel when it arrives. If the packaging is damaged, note this with the courier if you can. If anything is damaged, wrong or missing, contact us and send photos. See our Returns page.

The goods stay at FashForge's risk until they are delivered to the address you gave us or you collect them from the locker. After that, the risk passes to you. If a brand partner sends the parcel, we remain responsible to you for loss or damage in transit. If the courier loses your parcel, we will replace the items (if available) or refund what you paid for them, including the delivery fee, to the original payment method as soon as we can and at most within 30 days.

7. If a piece sells out

If a piece is out of stock after you have ordered, we refund what you paid for that item, including any delivery fee that applied only to it, to the original payment method as soon as we can and at most within 30 days. See section 2 of our Returns page.
