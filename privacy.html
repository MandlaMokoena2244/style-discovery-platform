2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to answer you or to supply an order:

  • your name, phone number, and email address;
  • a delivery address in South Africa, if you order;
  • what you want to buy, including size and colour;
  • messages you send us on the contact form, email, or WhatsApp.

We do not ask for your card number. If a product has a payment link, you pay on that provider's page. We do not see or store your card details.

This website does not run advertising trackers. The contact form is sent to the service that hosts the form so we can read your message.

3. Why we use it

We use this information to:

  • reply to questions;
  • take and deliver an order you asked for;
  • handle a return or a complaint;
  • keep records we are required to keep.

The usual grounds under POPIA are that you asked us to do something (a contract), that we have a legitimate reason to run the shop, or that the law requires us to keep a record.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with people who need it to do their job for your order:

  • the courier, which may be the brand or FashForge, so the parcel can be delivered;
  • a payment provider, only when you use a payment link, and only as that provider needs;
  • the host of our contact form, so your message reaches us.

We do not send marketing messages.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order and message records only as long as we need them for your order and for the records South African law requires. We do not keep them longer than that for our own marketing.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it where the law allows. You can also object to a use that is not required to complete your order. Email hello@fashforge.co.za or WhatsApp us.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

7. Children

Kids' outfits are bought by an adult. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child for its own account.
