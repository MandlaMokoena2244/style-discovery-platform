2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to answer you or to supply an order:

  • your name and contact details (phone number and email address);
  • your delivery address in South Africa;
  • your order history, including what you bought, the size, and the delivery choice;
  • a payment confirmation from the payment provider, such as that a payment succeeded. We do not collect or store your card number;
  • messages you send us on the contact form, by email, or on WhatsApp.

3. Why we use it

We use this information to reply to you, take and deliver an order, handle a return or a complaint, and keep the records the law requires.

Under POPIA, we do this because you asked us to supply the goods (a contract), because we have a legitimate reason to run the shop and answer you, or because the law requires us to keep a record. We do not send marketing emails or WhatsApp messages unless you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only so an order or a message can be dealt with:

  • the courier who delivers the parcel;
  • the Courier Guy locker service, when you choose a locker;
  • the brand partner that sends the parcel;
  • the payment provider that takes the payment, once that provider is named on the payment page. It is not confirmed on this website yet;
  • Web3Forms, a form delivery service, which processes contact form messages and delivers them to hello@fashforge.co.za.

Web3Forms, and in some cases the payment provider, may process this information outside South Africa, only to deliver your message or take your payment. We share a delivery name, address and phone number with a brand partner only when that brand sends your parcel, and only so it can be delivered.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking pixels. We have checked the pages and scripts: there is no analytics tag.

If you add something to the cart, this site keeps that cart in your browser's local storage under the name fashforge_cart, on that device only. It is not a cookie, and it is not sent to us until you place an order. You can clear it in your browser.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records, including the payment confirmation, for as long as South African company and tax law requires us to keep business records. We keep messages only while we need them to answer you or to deal with a complaint or a claim. We do not keep information for marketing.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it where the law allows. We do not send marketing emails or WhatsApp messages unless you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time. You can also object to a use that is not needed to complete your order. Email hello@fashforge.co.za or WhatsApp +27 67 256 5980. We will not charge you for a reasonable request.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

8. Children

Kids' outfits are bought by an adult. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child for the child's own account.
