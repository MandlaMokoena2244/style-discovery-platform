Returns

Effective date: These terms apply immediately. | Website: https://fashforge.co.za

We want you to love your outfit. This policy explains when you can return something and how refunds work. It forms part of our Terms and Conditions and does not reduce any of your rights under the Consumer Protection Act (CPA) or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECT Act).

Also see our Terms and Conditions and Shipping.

Item sold out after you paid
Your choice: another size or piece, a refund of that item, or a refund of the whole outfit.
Changed your mind
Online orders can be cancelled in the cooling-off period set by the ECT Act. We do not promise a longer change-of-mind return.
Wrong, damaged or not as described
Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act apply. Contact us and we will repair, replace, or refund as the Act requires.
How refunds are paid
Back the way you paid, within the time the CPA or the ECT Act requires.

1. When we cancel an order

We may cancel all or part of an order if a piece is no longer available, there is an obvious pricing or description mistake, we suspect fraud, or we cannot deliver to your address. If we cancel, we will tell you why and refund what you paid for the cancelled part in full. We will not charge you any cancellation fee.

2. If a piece sells out after you have paid

A piece can still sell out after you have paid. If this happens, we will contact you and you can choose one of these:

  1. An alternative. We suggest another size or a similar piece. If the price is different, we tell you first. You pay the difference or we refund it. You can say no.
  2. A refund of the unavailable item(s). We send the rest of your order as normal.
  3. A refund of the whole outfit. If the missing piece is part of a full outfit and you would rather not go ahead, we refund the whole outfit, including any delivery fee.

If we cannot reach you, we refund the unavailable item.

3. Changed your mind

If you bought from this website, you may cancel during the cooling-off period in the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act. We do not offer a longer change-of-mind return unless we agree it with you. Goods that are defective, the wrong item, or not what we described are covered separately, under the Consumer Protection Act.

4. Wrong, damaged or missing items

Please check your parcel when it arrives. If something is wrong, damaged, missing, or not what you ordered, contact us and send photos of the item, the label, and the packaging. Keep the item unused, with its tags, while we sort it out. Where the Consumer Protection Act requires it, we will repair or replace the item, or refund you, and we will cover the reasonable cost of sending it back.

5. How to return something

  1. WhatsApp us on +27 67 256 5980 or send a query through the contact form on our website with your order number, the item(s), the reason, and photos if the item is damaged or wrong.
  2. We will confirm your return. Send it to 1064 Cornelius Street, Weltevredenpark, Roodepoort, Gauteng, 1709 unless we give you a different address.
  3. Pack the item(s) securely and include your order number.
  4. Send the parcel with a courier that gives you a tracking number, and keep your proof of sending.

6. How and when refunds are paid

Refunds go back the way you paid. If you paid by card through a payment link, the refund goes back to that card. We do not see or store your card number.

We send the refund within the time the Consumer Protection Act or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act requires. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.

We do not give refunds in cash.

7. When we may refuse a return

We may refuse a return that the Consumer Protection Act and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act do not require, for example a change of mind after the cooling-off period, or an item that was used or damaged after delivery.
