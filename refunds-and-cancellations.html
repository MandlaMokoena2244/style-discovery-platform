Returns

Last updated: 21 November 2026. | Website: www.fashforge.co.za

We want you to love your outfit. This policy explains when you can return something and how refunds work. It forms part of our Terms and Conditions and does not reduce any of your rights under the Consumer Protection Act (CPA) or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECT Act).

Also see our Terms and Conditions and Shipping.

Item sold out after you ordered
We refund what you paid for that item, including any delivery fee that applied only to it, as soon as we can and at most within 30 days.
Cooling-off (section 44 of the ECT Act)
Cancel within 7 days of the day you receive the goods, without a reason or a penalty. We refund what you paid, including the original delivery fee, within 30 days. You pay only the direct cost of sending the goods back. Please return items unworn, unwashed, with tags and original packaging.
Faulty, defective or incorrect
Tell us as soon as you can. For six months, section 56 of the CPA lets you choose a repair, a replacement, or a refund. FashForge pays the delivery. The refund, if you choose one, is paid as soon as we can and at most within 30 days.
How refunds are paid
To the original payment method. A cooling-off refund includes the original delivery fee and is paid within 30 days of cancellation.

1. When we cancel an order

We may cancel all or part of an order if a piece is no longer available, there is an obvious pricing or description mistake, we suspect fraud, or we cannot deliver to your address. If we cancel, we will tell you why. We will not charge you any cancellation fee.

If we refund you because an item sold out, was cancelled by us, or was faulty or incorrect, we refund what you paid for that item, including any delivery fee that applied only to it, to the original payment method as soon as we can and at most within 30 days.

2. If a piece sells out after you have ordered

If an item is out of stock after you have ordered and you would rather take another size or a similar piece, tell us. We will only do that if you agree.

If we refund you because an item sold out, was cancelled by us, or was faulty or incorrect, we refund what you paid for that item, including any delivery fee that applied only to it, to the original payment method as soon as we can and at most within 30 days.

3. Cooling-off: you changed your mind

Under section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, you may cancel within 7 days after you receive the goods. You do not have to give a reason, and we do not charge a penalty.

If you cancel in the 7-day cooling-off period (counted from the day you receive the goods), we refund what you paid, including the original delivery fee, within 30 days of cancellation, to the same payment method. The only cost you bear is the direct cost of sending the goods back. Please return items unworn, unwashed, with tags and original packaging. We will not refuse a return that the ECT Act or the Consumer Protection Act requires.

Tell us on WhatsApp or through the contact form as soon as you decide to return, with your order details and the item.

Nothing on this page limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act or the ECT Act.

4. Faulty, defective, wrong or missing items

Please tell us as soon as you can if something is faulty, defective, wrong, missing, or not what you ordered, and send photos of the item, the label, and the packaging.

Section 56 of the Consumer Protection Act gives you an implied warranty of quality for six months. You choose a repair, a replacement, or a refund. FashForge pays the delivery cost of the return and of a replacement. We do not ask you to pay those delivery costs. If you choose a refund, we refund what you paid for that item, including any delivery fee that applied only to it, to the original payment method as soon as we can and at most within 30 days.

A product is not treated as faulty only because of normal wear, an alteration, damage from not following the care instructions, or a natural feature of the fabric, such as slubs in linen. This does not affect your cooling-off right or your rights if the item is actually defective or not as described.

5. How to return something

  1. Contact us as soon as you decide to return. WhatsApp us on +27 67 256 5980 or send a query through the contact form on our website with your order number, the item(s), the reason, and photos if the item is damaged or wrong.
  2. We will confirm your return. Send it to 1064 Cornelius Street, Weltevredenpark, Roodepoort, Gauteng, 1709 unless we give you a different address. For some parcels sent directly by a brand, we may give you a different return address so the item can go back to the brand.
  3. Return the items with their tags and the original packaging. Pack them securely and include your order number.
  4. Send the parcel with a courier that gives you a tracking number, and keep your proof of sending.

For a cooling-off return, you pay the direct cost of sending the goods back. For a faulty or incorrect item, FashForge pays the delivery.

6. How and when refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We do not see or store your card number, and we do not refund in cash. Your bank may take a little longer to show a refund.

If you cancel in the 7-day cooling-off period (counted from the day you receive the goods), we refund what you paid, including the original delivery fee, within 30 days of cancellation, to the same payment method. The only cost you bear is the direct cost of sending the goods back. Please return items unworn, unwashed, with tags and original packaging.
