Returns

Last updated: 20 November 2026. | Website: www.fashforge.co.za

We want you to love your outfit. This policy explains when you can return something and how refunds work. It forms part of our Terms and Conditions and does not reduce any of your rights under the Consumer Protection Act (CPA) or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECT Act).

Also see our Terms and Conditions and Shipping.

Item sold out after you ordered
A full refund.
Cooling-off (section 44 of the ECT Act)
Cancel within 7 days after you receive the goods, without a reason or a penalty. Return the items unworn, unwashed, and with tags. You pay the direct cost of the return delivery.
Faulty, defective or incorrect
Tell us as soon as you can. For six months, section 56 of the CPA lets you choose a repair, a replacement, or a refund. FashForge pays the delivery.
How refunds are paid
To the original payment method. A cooling-off refund is paid within 30 days of cancellation.

1. When we cancel an order

We may cancel all or part of an order if a piece is no longer available, there is an obvious pricing or description mistake, we suspect fraud, or we cannot deliver to your address. If we cancel, we will tell you why and refund what you paid for the cancelled part in full. We will not charge you any cancellation fee.

2. If a piece sells out after you have ordered

If an item is out of stock after you have ordered, we refund you in full for that item. If you would rather take another size or a similar piece, tell us. We will only do that if you agree.

3. Cooling-off: you changed your mind

Under section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, you may cancel within 7 days after you receive the goods. You do not have to give a reason, and we do not charge a penalty. The only amount you pay is the direct cost of returning the goods.

Tell us on WhatsApp or through the contact form, with your order details and the item. Send the items back unworn, unwashed, and with their tags.

We refund you within 30 days of the cancellation, to the original payment method. That timing is section 44(4) of the ECT Act.

Nothing on this page limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act or the ECT Act.

4. Faulty, defective, wrong or missing items

Please tell us as soon as you can if something is faulty, defective, wrong, missing, or not what you ordered, and send photos of the item, the label, and the packaging.

Section 56 of the Consumer Protection Act gives you an implied warranty of quality for six months. You choose a repair, a replacement, or a refund. FashForge pays the delivery cost of the return and of a replacement. We do not ask you to pay those delivery costs.

5. How to return something

  1. WhatsApp us on +27 67 256 5980 or send a query through the contact form on our website with your order number, the item(s), the reason, and photos if the item is damaged or wrong.
  2. We will confirm your return. Send it to 1064 Cornelius Street, Weltevredenpark, Roodepoort, Gauteng, 1709 unless we give you a different address.
  3. Pack the item(s) securely and include your order number.
  4. Send the parcel with a courier that gives you a tracking number, and keep your proof of sending.

For a cooling-off return, you pay the return delivery. For a faulty or incorrect item, FashForge pays the delivery.

6. How and when refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We do not see or store your card number, and we do not refund in cash.

If you cancel in the 7-day cooling-off period, we pay the refund within 30 days of your cancellation. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.

7. When we may refuse a return

We may refuse a cooling-off return if you tell us more than 7 days after you received the goods, or if the item has been worn or washed, or is missing its tags.

We will not refuse a return that the Consumer Protection Act or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act requires. Nothing here takes those rights away.
