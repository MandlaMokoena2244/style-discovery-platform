10. Cooling-off, returns and faulty goods

Under section 44 of the ECT Act, you may cancel an online order within 7 days after you receive the goods, without giving a reason and without a penalty.

If you cancel in the 7-day cooling-off period (counted from the day you receive the goods), we refund what you paid, including the original delivery fee, within 30 days of cancellation, to the same payment method. The only cost you bear is the direct cost of sending the goods back. Please return items unworn, unwashed, with tags and original packaging. We will not refuse a return that the ECT Act or the Consumer Protection Act requires.

If goods are faulty, defective, or not what you ordered, please tell us as soon as you can and send photos. Section 56 of the CPA gives you an implied warranty of quality for six months. You may choose a repair, a replacement, or a refund. FashForge pays the delivery cost for faulty or incorrect items, both for the return and for a replacement if we send one.

A product is not treated as faulty only because of normal wear, an alteration, damage from not following the care instructions, or a natural feature of the fabric, such as slubs in linen. This does not affect your cooling-off right or your rights if the item is actually defective or not as described.

More detail is on our Returns page. Nothing in these terms, or on that page, limits your rights under the CPA or the ECT Act.
